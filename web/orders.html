About protective stops & targets, and what the columns mean

When a buy fills, Claire leaves two orders at the broker to guard the shares — they work even when Claire, this PC or the internet is down:

  • 🛑 Stop-loss — sells the shares if the price falls to the stop price, so a loss cannot keep growing. Once the stop price is hit it sells at the market, so the fill can be a little below it (CSCO's 96.97 stop filled at 96.90).
  • 🎯 Take-profit — sells the shares if the price rises to the target, locking in the gain. It never sells below the target.
  • They are a pair: when one fills, the broker cancels the other.

Every buy is sent to Saxo together with its stop (and target, if set) in one request. (SGX on the Saxo SIM account and the built-in sims are simulated by Claire: there Claire's sim holds the stop-loss and take-profit and fills them when the live price reaches them — checked every 5 minutes while the market is open, so a fast fall can sell below the stop.) Until the buy fills they are attached; once it fills they are working. A buy still queued here shows its stop-loss and take-profit with the order.

Price the purchase or sale price, stop-loss, take-profit or trigger price · Distance how far the price has to move from the last price to reach it · Value / outcome shares × price, or for a stop/target the profit or loss on those shares versus what you paid (or the limit, before the buy fills), before fees.

To change them, sell the position (Portfolio, Saxo accounts or Watchlist — that cancels them first) or change them on Saxo's platform.
